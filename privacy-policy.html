Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 12, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Titan Classes ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Titan Classes mobile application and website at titanclasses.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

We do not sell your personal data to any third party, ever.

1. Information We Collect

When you register or use Titan Classes, we may collect the following:

  • Identity information: Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Location information: Your pincode and service area
  • Professional information (teachers): Subjects taught, class levels, availability, qualifications, and mode of teaching
  • Payment information: Transaction records for the teacher registration fee (payment processing is handled securely by Razorpay — we do not store card or UPI details)
  • Device information: Push notification tokens to send you relevant alerts
  • Usage data: App activity such as leads viewed and applications submitted

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Match teachers with relevant tuition leads in your area
  • Send you push notifications about new leads, demo schedules, and application updates
  • Process and verify teacher registrations and payments
  • Communicate with you via WhatsApp or phone about your account
  • Improve and operate our platform

3. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • With parents: When a teacher is shortlisted for a lead, limited profile details are shared with the parent
  • With Razorpay: For secure payment processing of the teacher registration fee
  • With Expo (push notifications): Device tokens are shared with Expo's push notification service
  • With Firebase: For notification delivery via Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect our users

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Supabase infrastructure with row-level security controls. We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data transmission. Access to your data is restricted to authorized Titan Classes team members only.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time via your device settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at [email protected].

6. Children's Privacy

Titan Classes is intended for use by teachers and parents only. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Student information (class, subjects) is provided by parents and is used only for matching purposes.

7. Advertising

We may occasionally show advertisements within the Titan Classes app. These ads may be from Titan Classes or third-party advertisers and may not always be related to education. We may use basic profile information such as gender or location to show you more relevant ads. We do not sell your personal data to advertisers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of any significant changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
